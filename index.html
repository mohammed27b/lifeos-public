PERSONAL OPERATIONS APPLICATION

LifeOS Personal

LifeOS is a private application for organizing personal activities and reviewing store operations. Its Stores module brings product, inventory, sales, advertising and financial reports together to help its owner understand results and plan actions.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The owner connects Google Drive so LifeOS can discover and read reports within the configured store-data folder. LifeOS uses read-only Drive access to prepare reports and explain operational decisions. The application's configured folder boundary limits its normal workflow; the OAuth permission itself may authorize broader read access.

LifeOS does not use this connection to modify or delete Google Drive files. Authorization credentials are kept in the owner's local environment, outside the application source repository.

Private application, public information

This website provides information about LifeOS and its data practices. It does not provide access to the private application, connected Google account or store reports.

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, retention and revocation details.
