Privacy policy

This policy describes the current private, owner-operated LifeOS Personal application and its read-only Google Drive connection.

Information accessed

With the owner's authorization, LifeOS reads file metadata and report contents from the configured Google Drive store-data area. Reports may contain product identifiers, inventory, sales, advertising and financial information, and any personal information present in the source reports. Only information needed for the selected workflow should be included.

Purpose and use

Data is used to match products, validate report sources, calculate operational metrics and prepare reports and decision summaries for the owner. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. The current Drive reporting workflow does not send report contents to an AI service.

Storage and access

Original files remain in the owner's chosen storage. LifeOS maintains local indexes, caches and generated reports as needed. OAuth credentials are stored locally outside the source repository. Browser caches may persist until cleared. Files placed in an owner-configured synchronized folder may also be synchronized by that storage service.

The owner controls the computer, account and storage used by LifeOS. OAuth credentials and private reports are not published on this website or included in the public source for these informational pages.

Sharing

LifeOS does not sell Google user data or disclose it to third parties for their independent use. Google processes authorization and Drive API requests. Owner-selected storage or backup providers may process files that the owner chooses to store with them.

Retention and deletion

Local credentials, caches, indexes and generated reports remain until the owner removes them. The owner can delete local application data and independently remove originals or backups from their selected storage. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously stored copies.

Revoke access

The owner can revoke LifeOS access through Google Account connections, and remove the locally stored OAuth credentials. Contact the operator for assistance with locating and deleting application data.

Google API data

LifeOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

These static informational pages do not ask for Google credentials or contain application analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information to serve and secure the website.

Contact and changes
